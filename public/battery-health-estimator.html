Battery Longevity Guidelines

Optimal 20% - 80% Rule Keep lithium-ion batteries between 20% and 80% to significantly reduce electrode stress and extend total charge cycle capacity.
Thermal Management Avoid charging in environments above 35°C (95°F). High heat during fast-charging degrades chemical cell health faster.
