Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Zero-Upload Architecture

Infiniteforge operates on a strict zero-upload model. All document processing (including PDF merging, splitting, compressing, and conversions), image editing, code formatting, video operations, and data sanitization occur 100% locally within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and JavaScript APIs.

Your files are never transmitted to our servers or stored on remote databases.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or view your uploaded images, PDFs, videos, or spreadsheet data.
  • We do not record private keys, passwords, or personal credentials entered into any encryption/JWT utility.
  • We do not require account registrations or store user profiles.

3. Local Storage

Certain productivity tools (such as Habit Trackers or Theme Preferences) utilize your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save settings across sessions. This data remains on your local machine and can be cleared anytime via browser settings.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We use standard privacy-respecting Google Analytics scripts solely to evaluate aggregate page visit statistics and improve site responsiveness. No personally identifiable information (PII) is captured.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@infiniteforge.tech.
